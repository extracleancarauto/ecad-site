      Before you book

      Quick questions

      How does the deposit work?

      A deposit secures your date and time slot, based on your vehicle's condition. It comes straight off your final total — it's a reservation, not an extra fee. The remaining balance is due on completion, paid by cash, bank transfer, or card.

      Why do prices vary instead of being fixed?

      Pricing reflects your vehicle's actual condition, not just its class — a well-maintained sedan and a heavily soiled one aren't the same job. This keeps pricing fair to everyone rather than everyone paying an inflated flat rate to cover worst-case vehicles.

      What do you need from me on the day?

      Access to a standard power outlet on-site for our equipment — that's the one thing we can't supply ourselves. We run a fully waterless / rinseless process with our own products, and carry a small self-sourced (non-NWC) water reserve for heavily soiled vehicles only.

      How does waterless detailing actually work?

      We apply a SiO2-infused waterless wash product directly to each panel, then work it panel-by-panel with clean, dedicated microfibre towels to lift dirt and grime without a hose or bucket rinse. For heavier soiling, we use a rinseless technique — a lubricating solution that suspends dirt for a safe wipe-down, using only a small reserve of self-sourced water that never touches the NWC supply.

      Do you travel outside Kingston & St. Andrew?

      Yes — Kingston, St. Andrew, and Portmore at standard pricing. St. Catherine and St. Thomas are served too, with a travel surcharge confirmed at booking, never a surprise on the day.

      What if there's already damage on my vehicle?

      Every job is photographed before and after. Any pre-existing damage is noted and pointed out to you before work begins — so there's never any ambiguity about what was already there.

      Can I cancel or reschedule?

      Your deposit is refundable with 24+ hours notice. Same-day cancellations or no-shows are non-refundable, since that slot could have gone to another client.
