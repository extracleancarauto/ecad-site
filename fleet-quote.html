Section 02 · Before you decide

What you should know upfront

Straight answers to the questions every fleet decision-maker asks before signing.

Coverage & liability

Commercial insurance conversion is currently in progress. Until completed, work is carried out with the same care and accountability we apply to every vehicle — full pre-service condition documentation and photos protect both parties. We'll confirm in writing once commercial coverage is active.

Payment terms

Consolidated monthly invoicing, due within 7 days of receipt. Cash, bank transfer, or WiPay accepted. GCT receipts available on request for Tier C accounts. No deposit required to receive your initial quote.

Contract flexibility

Month-to-month for Tier A & B — cancel anytime with 14 days notice. Tier C annual rate lock-in includes a 30-day mutual exit clause. No long-term lock-in required to get started.
